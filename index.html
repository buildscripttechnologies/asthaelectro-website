Our Process

Disciplined Workflow. Fast Iterations.

A repeatable four-phase process refined across 200+ programs to eliminate rework and accelerate time-to-prototype.

01

Discovery

Define electrical, mechanical, thermal, and compliance requirements. Agree on stack-up, layer count, and tools.

02

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What information do you need to start a project?

We typically need a functional/block diagram or initial schematic, mechanical constraints (board outline, connector positions, height limits), layer count preference, target fab house if known, and your estimated quantity for BOM optimization. We'll guide you through a discovery checklist at kickoff.

Which EDA tools do you work with?

We primarily use Altium Designer and KiCad, with experience in Cadence Allegro and OrCAD. We can deliver native project files or export to your preferred format. File migration between platforms is part of our service offering.

How long does a typical PCB design project take?

Simple 2–4 layer boards typically complete in 1–2 weeks. Complex 8–16 layer high-speed or RF designs range from 3–6 weeks depending on review cycles and scope changes. We provide a detailed timeline at kickoff and flag risks proactively.

Do you provide design files or just manufacturing outputs?

You receive everything: native EDA project files (schematics, PCB layout), manufacturing outputs (Gerbers, ODB++, IPC-2581), BOM, assembly drawings, and pick-and-place files. All intellectual property is fully transferred to you upon project completion.

Can you help with prototype bring-up and debugging?

Yes. We offer post-handoff engineering support including bring-up assistance, schematic review against measured behavior, and revision planning if issues are found during prototype testing.

Do you work with overseas or international clients?

Absolutely. We work with clients worldwide. All communication, file exchange, and reviews happen over video calls, shared cloud drives, and project management tools convenient for your time zone and workflow.
